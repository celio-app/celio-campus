Getting started

Setting up takes about five minutes, and you only do it once a semester.

  1. Make your account. Tap Sign Up, enter your name, email and a password. If it asks you to confirm your email, check your inbox (and junk folder).
  2. Add your semester. Give it a name like "Semester 1, 2026–27" and its start and end dates.
  3. Add your courses. One for each class, with the course code (like ENG2D) and your teacher's name if you like. Each course gets its own colour.
  4. Add what's due. Tap ＋ (or Add on a computer). Or save time and import your due dates.

What to fill in for each assignment

  • Title, course and due date. The only must-haves.
  • Type: assignment, test, quiz, project, lab and so on.
  • Start date: when you should begin. It shows a Start now tag once that day arrives, so big projects don't sneak up on you.
  • Time guess: roughly how long it will take. Semstr uses this to plan your week.
  • First step: the very first small thing to do, like "write the intro paragraph". Starting is the hardest part; this makes it easier.
  • Marks and weight: what it's out of and how much it counts. Add your mark later to see your grade.

Importing due dates

If your teachers post due dates in SLATE, Brightspace, Blackboard or Google Classroom, you can bring them all into Semstr at once instead of typing them in. It's the fastest way to set up a new semester: Semstr creates your courses from the file. Look for Import due dates on Today when you start a semester, or on the Calendar any time.

Step 1: get the calendar file

From SLATE or Brightspace

  1. Open Calendar in Brightspace.
  2. Open Settings, tick Enable Calendar Feeds and click Save.
  3. Back on the Calendar, click Subscribe, then Download. You'll get a file ending in .ics.

From Blackboard

  1. Open Calendar in Blackboard.
  2. Open Settings (the gear) and choose Get external calendar link.
  3. Copy the link and paste it into your browser's address bar. Your browser downloads a file ending in .ics.

From Google Classroom (its due dates live in Google Calendar)

  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer.
  2. Click the gear, then Settings → Import & export → Export.
  3. Unzip the download. Inside is a .ics file for each calendar; pick the one for your class.

Step 2: bring it into Semstr

  1. Tap Import due dates on Today (when you start a semester) or on the Calendar.
  2. Tap Choose .ics file and pick the file.
  3. Semstr groups everything by course. For each group, choose which of your courses it belongs to, pick ＋ New course, or choose Don't import this group.
  4. Untick anything you don't want. Items already in Semstr are greyed out, so you won't get doubles.
  5. Tap Import. Nothing is added until you do.
Tip: Semstr only shows due dates at first. Tick Show other events too if you also want things like class times or when work opens.
Teachers added or changed something? Download a fresh file and import it again. Semstr only adds what's new, skips what you already have, and shows any changes, like Date changed: Nov 20 → Nov 24. Those update the assignment you already have, so your progress and grades stay. If something says Moved?, tick it if it's the same assignment.

The Today screen

Today is your home base. Top to bottom:

  • The numbers bar: what's Late, Due today, Due this week, how much is Done this term, and your Grade average. Tap any number to see that list.
  • Do this next: the one thing Semstr thinks you should work on now (late work first, then what's due soonest). Tap Start 25 min to begin a focus session, or Not now to see the next suggestion.
  • Today's plan: study sessions you've planned for today. See Planning your day.
  • The week strip: tap a day to see what's due then. Coloured dots show what's due; the bar under each day shows how much time you've planned.
  • Due this week: tick the box when you finish something. It moves out of the list and counts toward your streak. Tapped it by mistake? Tap Undo on the green message.

On a computer or tablet you'll also see your courses with their grades, your week's progress, today's habits and your badges.

Planning your day

Knowing what's due is half of it. The other half is deciding when you'll do it.

  1. Tap Plan today (on Today or the Calendar), or Plan this day on any calendar day.
  2. The first time, set My routine: things that happen most weeks, like school, practice or a job. The planner keeps those times free.
  3. Tap Suggest a plan for Semstr to fit short sessions around your routine, or add them yourself: pick an assignment from the list, then tap a time.
  4. Drag a session to move it. On a phone, press and hold first.
Tip: two or three short sessions a day is plenty. When a planned session's time comes, it shows up in Up next on Today.

Assignments and grades

  • The Assignments tab lists everything, with filters for To Do, In Progress, Done, Late and This Week, plus a search box.
  • Each due date is a little calendar page: red means late, yellow means today.
  • Tap any assignment to edit it.

Entering grades

When you get a mark back, type it into the Score box (for example 17 out of 20). If the assignment has a weight, Semstr works out your grade for that course and your overall average.

Gold stars: score 80% or higher and a star sticker lands on that day in your Calendar. 90% and up gets a bigger one.

Calendar

  • See the whole month. On a phone, each day shows coloured dots; tap a day to see the list.
  • Move a due date by dragging it to another day. On a phone, press and hold first.
  • Star stickers mark days you got an A.
  • Import due dates lives here too. See Importing due dates.

Timeline

The Timeline shows each piece of work as a bar running from its start date to its due date, grouped by course. It's the best way to spot a crowded week coming.

  • The Workload row adds up your time guesses for each week: green is fine, yellow is busy, red is heavy.
  • Drag a bar to move it, or drag either end to change just the start or due date. On a phone, press and hold first.
  • Tap a course name to fold its section away.

Courses

Each course card shows your grade so far, the next thing due, how much you've finished and anything late. Tap Open course to see all its work, or use the pencil and bin to edit or delete it.

Deleting a course also deletes its assignments.

Habits

Habits are small things you want to do most days, like reading for 20 minutes, drinking water or getting outside.

  • Tap ＋ New Habit, pick a sticker and a name.
  • Some habits have a count, like 6 glasses of water. Tap the circles as you go.
  • Tick off today's habits right from Today on a computer, or from the Habits tab.

Streaks, goals and badges

  • A day counts when you finish an assignment or do at least the minimum of a habit.
  • Your weekly goal is how many days a week you aim for. Tap Goal on the This week card to change it and to pick rest days, which never count against you.
  • Your streak counts weeks in a row where you hit your goal. Missing a week pauses it instead of wiping it out, so you can pick it back up.
  • Badges unlock as you go, like finishing work early or planning ahead. See them all in the Badges card.

Put Semstr on your phone

Semstr works like an app once it's on your home screen.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open semstr.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open semstr.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

Look for the yellow s. icon.

Colours, account and privacy

  • Colours and dark mode: tap the palette button at the top. Choose Light or Dark and one of seven colours.
  • Your account: tap Your account at the bottom of the side menu (☰ on a phone). There you can download a copy of your data or delete your account.
  • Semesters: tap the pencil next to a semester in the side menu to rename it or delete it when it's over.
  • Privacy: read what Semstr keeps and why.

Feedback and help

Something broken, confusing, or got an idea? Tap Feedback at the top of any screen. Choose what kind of note it is, write what happened, and tap Send. Semstr notes which screen you were on and what device you're using, so you don't have to explain that part.

You can also email hello@semstr.app.
