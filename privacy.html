Privacy

What Semstr keeps, why it keeps it, and how you stay in control.

The short version

  • Semstr keeps only what you add, so it can show you what's due and help you plan.
  • There are no ads, and your information is never sold.
  • You can download a copy of your data or delete your account at any time from Your account in the app.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, your password (stored scrambled, so no one can read it) and the name you choose.
  • Your schoolwork: semesters, courses, assignments, due dates, grades you enter and notes.
  • Your routines: habits, check-ins, planned study sessions, streaks and badges.
  • Settings: things like light or dark mode and your colour choice.
  • Sign-in devices: a random code for each device you sign in on, with a label like "Chrome on Windows", so we can email you if your account is used somewhere new.

Semstr doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos or phone number.

Why we keep it

Only to run the app for you: showing your work, planning your day, tracking habits and keeping your account secure. Your information isn't used for advertising, and it isn't shared with other students.

Where it's stored

  • Supabase stores your account and data in a secure database. Each student can only see their own information.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering. Your browser loads it from Google, which sees a normal web request but none of your Semstr data.

You're in control

  • Download: Your account → Your data → Download gives you a file with everything you've added.
  • Delete: Your account → Delete my account removes your account and everything in it. This can't be undone.
  • Change: you can edit or remove any course, assignment, grade or habit whenever you like.

Questions

If you or a parent or guardian has a question about your information, contact: hello@semstr.app
